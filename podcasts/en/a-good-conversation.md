Conversations that make a familiar route feel new.

## Listen for a while

Ideas sometimes arrive quietly through a conversation. Agreement is optional; listening long enough to understand is what matters.

## Something for the road

A short episode on a walk, or a long conversation for an unhurried afternoon.
