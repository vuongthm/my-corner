A place for sentences, ideas and feelings that linger after the last page.

## A page, a pause

Reading more does not always mean understanding more. Sometimes an idea deserves a little time, a note in the margin, and a place in everyday life.

## What stays with me

A sentence, another perspective, or a question that can wait.
